# "Superman" Certification
**Background**
All new Metrology Engineers and Technicians at Entegris complete the “Superman” certification to become qualified users of [**ZONE3**](https://www.ogpnet.com/products/software/metrology-software/zone3-metrology-software/), a coordinate measuring machine (CMM) software platform. According to my metrology supervisor, I was the fastest person to complete the certification, obtaining it **by my second week** in the co-op.

**Scope**
The certification is designed to develop fundamental CMM inspection skills, including part fixturing, CMM joystick operation, and measurement of features specified on a technical drawing (e.g., circle diameters, surface flatness, and heights). The standardized training part is a machined block resembling the Superman logo, hence the name “Superman” certification.

**Passing Requirements**
- Collect all 13 drawing-specified measurements (parameters) in **TWO** separate programs: 
    - one utilizing only touch probes
    - one utilizing only optics/vision

- Pass a [**Type 1 Gage Study**](https://support.minitab.com/en-us/minitab/help-and-how-to/quality-and-process-improvement/measurement-system-analysis/supporting-topics/other-gage-studies-and-measures/type-1-gage-study/) by executing each program for 21 separate measurement runs and ensuring the percent variation (%Var) for every measured parameter is ≤10%.

The $$ \%var $$ is defined as:
$$
\% var = \frac{6*\sigma_{p}}{T} \times 100\%
$$
where:
- $$\sigma_{p}= $$ the **sample standard deviation** of the 21 measurements for a parameter 
- $$T = $$ tolerance defined as $$T= \text{upper spec limit} - \text{lower spec limit}$$
