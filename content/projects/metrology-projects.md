# CMM Programming Certification
**Background**
All new Metrology Engineers and Technicians at Entegris complete the company's certification to become qualified users of [ZONE3](https://www.ogpnet.com/products/software/metrology-software/zone3-metrology-software/), a coordinate measuring machine (CMM) software platform. According to my supervisor, I was the fastest person to complete the first half of the certification, obtaining it **by my second week** in the co-op. Currently, I am training a new metrology technician on the same software!

**Scope**
The certification is designed to develop fundamental CMM inspection skills, including part fixturing, CMM joystick operation, and measurement of features specified on a technical drawing (e.g., circle diameters, surface flatness, and heights). 

**Passing Requirements**
- Collect all drawing-specified measurements in **TWO** separate programs for the training part: 
    - one utilizing only touch probes
    - one utilizing only optics/vision

- Pass a [Type 1 Gage Study](https://support.minitab.com/en-us/minitab/help-and-how-to/quality-and-process-improvement/measurement-system-analysis/supporting-topics/other-gage-studies-and-measures/type-1-gage-study/) on the training part by ensuring the percent variation (%Var) for every measured parameter is ≤10%.

- Write and qualify a CMM program on an **actual part used in Entegris' products**:
    - Pass a [Gage Reproducibility & Repeatability (Gage R&R) Study](https://support.minitab.com/en-us/minitab/help-and-how-to/quality-and-process-improvement/measurement-system-analysis/how-to/gage-study/crossed-gage-r-r-study/before-you-start/overview/)
    - Format and formally present results as part of Management of Change (MOC) process
