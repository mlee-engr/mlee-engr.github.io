# Why Should You Hire Me?

I want to see designs through each step to their final stage — not just hand off a drawing. My biggest weakness is limited industry experience, but I make up for it with my ability to quickly learn and become proficient in whatever I put my mind to: I joined Entegris in July knowing nothing about quality or metrology, and by August, I was contributing to high-stakes projects and training new metrology technicians.

For me, learning by immersion (**especially by talking with engineers and technicians**) while facing real problems head-on beats studying a textbook front to back any day.
